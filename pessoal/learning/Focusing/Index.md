# English Study Pack — Cambridge (B2/C1) Prep

Built from your "Things to fix.md" list. Six files, meant to be used in this order:

1. **01_Study_Guide.md** — every grammar point on your list, explained, with your recurring errors flagged.
2. **02_Diagnostic_Quiz.md** — 50 questions to find exactly where the gaps are, before you study.
3. **03_Exam_Focus_Predictions.md** — where each topic is most likely to appear in Cambridge B2 First / C1 Advanced and in IELTS.
4. **04_Mock_Exam.md** — a full timed Use of English + Writing paper.
5. **05_Flashcards.md** — Obsidian-ready flashcards (works with the Spaced Repetition plugin, `Front::Back` format).
6. Answer keys are at the bottom of files 02 and 04, clearly separated — don't scroll down until you've finished.

## Suggested order of attack

1. Do the **diagnostic quiz cold** (no notes) — this is the most useful data point you'll get.
2. Grade yourself, mark which topics you missed.
3. Read only _those_ sections of the Study Guide in depth (skim the rest).
4. Drill the flashcards for a few days.
5. Sit the mock exam under time pressure.
6. Re-read the Study Guide sections for anything you still got wrong.

## Your known recurring errors (from past sessions — prioritise these)

- **this/that + plural nouns** (should be these/those)
- **double comparatives** (e.g. "more better")
- **irregular passives** (irregular past participles used wrong in passive voice)
- **countable/uncountable noun distinctions**

These four get extra attention throughout the pack, not just in their "own" sections.
