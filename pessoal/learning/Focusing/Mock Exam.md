## Section 1: Key Word Transformation (10 items, worth the most — this is your highest-leverage skill)

Complete the second sentence so it means the same as the first, using the given word. Do not change the given word. Use between 3 and 6 words.

1. "I broke the window," Tom admitted. (BROKEN) Tom admitted _______________________ the window.
    
2. It wasn't necessary for you to bring anything. (NEEDN'T) You _______________________ anything.
    
3. People believe the company lost millions last year. (BELIEVED) The company _______________________ millions last year.
    
4. I'm sure she didn't forget the meeting; she mentioned it yesterday. (CAN'T) She _______________________ the meeting.
    
5. Although it was raining heavily, they continued the match. (SPITE) _______________________ heavy rain, they continued the match.
    
6. If you don't submit the form, your application will be rejected. (UNLESS) Your application will be rejected _______________________ the form.
    
7. Maria, not John, solved the case. (WHO) It _______________________ solved the case, not John.
    
8. He regrets not studying law. (WISH) He _______________________ law.
    
9. She is more intelligent than her brother by far. (FAR) She is _______________________ intelligent than her brother.
    
10. They will announce the results next week. (ANNOUNCED) The results _______________________ next week.

## Section 5: Writing — Choose ONE (220–260 words, 30–35 minutes)

**Option A — Essay (formal/academic register):** "Some people believe that free legal aid clinics, like university-based Núcleos de Prática Jurídica, are essential for access to justice, while others argue governments should fund public defenders instead. Discuss both views and give your own opinion."

_Try to include, naturally, at least: one passive construction, one cleft sentence, one participle clause, and one hypothetical/conditional structure._

**Option B — Formal email (matches your real NPJ context):** Write a formal email to a Cartório de Registro de Imóveis, on behalf of a client, requesting information about the required documentation for averbação de quitação, and formally requesting the fee waiver (gratuidade de emolumentos) procedure be applied given the client's economic situation.

_Try to include, naturally, at least: reported speech (referencing what the client told you), a formal reporting verb pattern, and passive voice for procedural description._
