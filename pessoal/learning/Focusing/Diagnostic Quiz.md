### A. Comparatives & Superlatives [§1] (Q1–4)

1. This exercise is _______ (difficult) than the last one. 
> *less difficult*
2. She is by far _______ (good) student in the class.
> *the best*
3. Correct or incorrect? "This laptop is more faster than my old one." — fix it if wrong.
> *Wrong, "faster" doesn't need "more", it already brings the necessary context of more. (Is faster than)*
4. The _______ (much) you practice, the _______ (confident) you become.
> *more / more confident*

### B. Adjectives ending in -ly [§2] (Q5–6)

5. Choose the correct option: She smiled at me in a ==_friendly== / friendlily_ way.
6. True or false: "lonely" can be used as an adverb modifying a verb (e.g. "he walked lonely").
> *False, for this to work you would need to put "lonely" before walked, modifying "he". "ly" words can't be adverbs*

### C. Reported Speech [§3] (Q7–12)

7. "I'll call you tomorrow," he said. → He said _______.
> He said that he would call him/me tomorrow.
8. "Where do you live?" she asked me. → She asked me _______.
> She asked me where do I live.
9. "Don't touch that," the teacher said to the students. → The teacher told the students _______.
> The teacher told the students/them to not touch that. 
10. Fill the gap with the correct preposition: She apologised _______ being late.
> *For*
11. "It was me who broke the vase," he admitted. → He admitted _______ the vase.
> *He admitted to breaking the vase*
12. Fill the gap: The lawyer insisted _______ (see) the original document.
> *seeing*
### D. Conditionals [§4] (Q13–18)

13. If I _______ (know) about the meeting, I would have attended.
> *knew*
14. If she weren't so busy, she _______ (help) you now.
> *would have helped* - X
15. Unless you _______ (submit) the form by Friday, your application won't be considered.
> *submit*
16. Name the conditional type: "If it rains, we cancel the picnic." (general truth) 
> *zero conditional*
17. Rewrite using "provided that": "You can leave early if you finish the report." →
> *Provided that the report is finished, you can leave early (I think this need another condition to work)
> *Provided that you finish the report, you can leave early*
18. Mixed conditional: If he hadn't missed the flight, he _______ (be) here now.
> *would be*

### E. Possessives [§5] (Q19–22)

19. Correct or incorrect? "I don't like this kind of people." — fix if wrong.
> *Incorrect, These*
20. Correct or incorrect? "Give me those book." — fix if wrong.
> *Incorrect, that*
21. Fill in: A friend _______ mine recommended this restaurant. (of/from)
> *of*
22. Fill in: I appreciate _______ (you) helping me with this.
> *you* -- X (WHY??)

### F. Hypothetical Clauses / Wish / If Only [§6] (Q23–26)

25. I wish he _______ (stop) making so much noise. (annoyance)
> *would stop*
26. It's time we _______ (leave).
> *left*
