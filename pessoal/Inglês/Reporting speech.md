Verbs to know:

say, tell, ask, explain, mention, wonder, think, believe, assume, suppose, insist, demand, order, warn, argue, claim, suggest, recommend, advise, propose, promise, agree, refuse, offer, threaten, admit, deny, complain, apologize, regret, urge


| Pattern                                     | Verbs                                                                             | Example                                                                        |
| ------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **1. verb + object + to-infinitive**        | warn, advise, urge, order, tell, ask                                              | She **warned** him **to be** careful with the contract.                        |
| *2. verb + (that) + should / subjunctive*   | suggest, recommend, propose, insist, demand, urge                                 | The professor **suggested** (that) the students **submit** their essays early. |
| **3. verb + object + preposition + gerund** | apologize (for), complain (about), warn (against)                                 | She **apologized for being** late.                                             |
| *4. verb + to-infinitive (no object)*       | agree, refuse, offer, threaten, promise, claim                                    | He **agreed to sign** the document.                                            |
| **5. verb + that-clause**                   | say, admit, deny, argue, claim, explain, mention, think, believe, assume, suppose | He **admitted that** he had made a mistake.                                    |
| *6. verb + gerund (-ing)*                   | deny, admit, suggest, recommend                                                   | He **denied signing** the form.                                                |

Note: pattern 2 uses the base form even for third person — _"suggested he submit,"_ not _"submits."_
