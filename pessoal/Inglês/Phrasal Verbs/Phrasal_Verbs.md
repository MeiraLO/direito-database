# Phrasal Verbs — Master List
> Grouped by particle · Organised for IELTS and Cambridge First

---

## How to Memorise Phrasal Verbs

1. **Learn in context** — always write a sentence, not just the definition
2. **Group by particle** (out, up, on, off, over, through, back, away, down)
3. **Notice if separable or inseparable:**
   - *Separable:* "Turn off the TV" OR "Turn the TV off" | "Turn it off" ✓ | NOT "Turn off it" ✗
   - *Inseparable:* "She came across an old letter" | NOT "She came an old letter across" ✗

## BY PARTICLE

### 🔵 OUT

| Phrasal Verb | Meaning | Sentence |
|---|---|---|
| find out | discover | I need to find out what time the bus leaves. |
| run out of | exhaust a supply | We've run out of time. |
| work out | calculate; train; resolve | Can you work out the answer? / She works out every morning. |
| carry out | perform / conduct | The team carried out the research. |
| set out | begin a journey; start | They set out before dawn. |
| give out | distribute; stop working | He gave out the handouts. / The engine gave out. |
| turn out | result; prove to be | It turned out to be a great film. |
| stand out | be conspicuous | Her talent really stands out. |
| come out | be published; become known | His novel comes out in March. |
| sort out | organise; resolve | We need to sort out the accommodation. |
| point out | indicate | She pointed out the mistake. |
| miss out (on) | not experience something | I missed out on the best part. |
| fall out with | argue and end a friendship | He fell out with his business partner. |
| figure out | understand; solve | I can't figure out why it's not working. |
| look out (for) | be careful; watch for | Look out for ice on the road. |
| wear out | exhaust; become unusable | These shoes are completely worn out. |
| put out | extinguish; inconvenience | They put out the fire quickly. |
| rule out | eliminate a possibility | The doctors ruled out cancer. |
| break out | escape; start suddenly | War broke out in the region. |
| phase out | gradually stop using | The government plans to phase out diesel cars. |

## Topic-Grouped Phrasal Verbs

### Health
- come down with (a cold) · get over (an illness) · build up (fitness) · cut out (unhealthy food) · take up (exercise) · burn off (calories)

### Study / Work
- catch up (with work) · fall behind (in studies) · go over (notes) · hand in (an assignment) · look up (a word) · write up (a report) · take on (a project)

### Relationships
- fall out with (sb) · make up (with sb) · look up to (sb) · put up with (sb) · get on with (sb) · break up (with sb) · grow apart

### Technology
- log in / log out · set up (an account) · shut down (a device) · back up (data) · plug in (a device) · switch on/off

### Money
- pay off (a debt) · save up (for sth) · cut back (on spending) · take out (a loan) · come into (money) · run out of (money)
