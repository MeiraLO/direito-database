# Irregular Verbs — Complete Guide

## 📌 When Do Verbs Become Irregular?

In English, verbs change form in two situations:

- **Past Simple** → instead of adding -ed, the verb changes its spelling (or stays the same).
- **Past Participle** → used in Present Perfect (_have/has + pp_) and Past Perfect (_had + pp_).

Regular verbs follow one rule for both: **base → base+ed → base+ed** Irregular verbs follow **no single rule** — each group has its own pattern.

---

## 🔷 Group 1 — All Three Forms Are the Same (Base = Past = Participle)

These verbs **never change**. The same word is used in all tenses.

|Base Form|Past Simple|Past Participle|Example|
|---|---|---|---|
|cut|cut|cut|I cut the paper. / I have cut the paper.|
|put|put|put|She put it here. / She had put it there.|
|hit|hit|hit|He hit the ball. / He has hit it twice.|
|let|let|let|They let me in. / They have let us down.|
|set|set|set|She set the table. / She had set it early.|
|shut|shut|shut|He shut the door. / He has shut it already.|
|split|split|split|It split in two. / It has split apart.|
|cost|cost|cost|It cost a lot. / It has cost us dearly.|
|hurt|hurt|hurt|She hurt her leg. / She has hurt herself.|
|read|read|read|I read it yesterday. / I have read it. ⚠️ pronunciation changes: /rɛd/|
|spread|spread|spread|It spread fast. / It has spread widely.|
|bet|bet|bet|He bet everything. / He has never bet before.|

> 💡 **Pattern:** These verbs mostly end in **-t** or **-d**. The spelling never changes, but sometimes the pronunciation does (like _read_).

|Group|Pattern|Example|
|---|---|---|
|**1** — All forms the same|base = past = participle|cut / cut / cut|
|**2** — Past = Participle|base ≠ past = participle|buy / bought / bought|
|**3** — All different|base ≠ past ≠ participle|go / went / gone|

## 🧠 When Does the Irregular Form Actually Apply?

|Situation|Form Used|Example|
|---|---|---|
|**Past Simple** (affirmative)|Irregular past form|_She **wrote** the document._|
|**Past Simple** (negative)|Base form (after _didn't_)|_She **didn't write** it._ ← back to base!|
|**Past Simple** (question)|Base form (after _did_)|_Did she **write** it?_ ← back to base!|
|**Present Perfect**|Past Participle (after _have/has_)|_She **has written** many documents._|
|**Past Perfect**|Past Participle (after _had_)|_She **had written** it before the deadline._|
|**Passive Voice**|Past Participle (after _be_)|_The document **was written** by her._|

> ⚠️ **Key rule:** The irregular form only appears in the **affirmative Past Simple** and whenever a **past participle** is needed (Present Perfect, Past Perfect, Passive). In negatives and questions with _did_, always use the **base form**.
