# Past Continuous

## 📌 What is the Past Continuous?

The Past Continuous describes an action that was **in progress at a specific moment in the past**. It emphasises duration and background context — not the start or end of the action.

---

## 📐 Structure

| Type | Structure | Example |
|---|---|---|
| ✅ Affirmative | Subject + **was/were** + verb**-ing** | They were studying all night. |
| ❌ Negative | Subject + **wasn't/weren't** + verb**-ing** | She wasn't sleeping at midnight. |
| ❓ Question | **Was/Were** + subject + verb**-ing**? | What were you doing at 8 PM? |

---

## 🧠 When to Use the Past Continuous

- Action **in progress at a specific past moment**.
  > *At 9 PM yesterday, I was reading a legal case.*
  > *She was cooking when I arrived.*

- A **background action interrupted** by a shorter Past Simple action.
  > *I was walking home when it started to rain.*
  > *They were having dinner when the phone rang.*

- Two actions **happening simultaneously** (with *while*).
  > *While she was writing the petition, he was reviewing the evidence.*
  > *I was studying while my roommate was watching TV.*

- **Setting the scene** in a narrative.
  > *It was raining. The streets were empty. A woman was standing by the door.*

---

## ⏰ Key Time Expressions

| Expression | Example |
|---|---|
| while | While I was studying, she was sleeping. |
| when | He called when I was having lunch. |
| at + time yesterday | At 6 AM yesterday, they were already working. |
| all morning / all day | She was practising English all morning. |
| at that moment / at that time | At that moment, everyone was watching the news. |

---

## 📝 Spelling Rules for -ing

- Most verbs: add **-ing** → read → read**ing**
- Verbs ending in silent **-e**: drop the e, add **-ing** → write → writ**ing**
- Short verbs (CVC): double the consonant → sit → sit**ting** | run → run**ning**
- Verbs ending in **-ie**: change to y → lie → ly**ing**

---

## ⚠️ Stative Verbs — NO Continuous Form

Some verbs are **never** used in a continuous form. These are called **stative verbs**:

| Category | Verbs |
|---|---|
| Mental states | know, believe, understand, think (opinion), remember, forget |
| Emotions | love, hate, like, prefer, want, need |
| Perception | see, hear, smell, taste, feel (involuntary) |
| Possession | have, own, belong, contain, include |

> ❌ *I was knowing the answer.*
> ✅ *I knew the answer.*

---

## 💬 Extended Examples

> ✅ *The lawyers were negotiating when the judge entered the room.*
> ✅ *While the defendant was speaking, the jury was taking notes.*
> ✅ *It was getting dark. The hospital corridors were filling with people.*
> ❓ *Were you expecting that decision from the court?*
> ❌ *The patient wasn't responding to the treatment at that point.*
