# Past Simple

## 📌 What is the Past Simple?

The Past Simple is used to talk about actions or events that were **completed at a specific time in the past**. The action is fully finished — it has no direct connection to the present moment.

---

## 📐 Structure

| Type | Structure | Example |
|---|---|---|
| ✅ Affirmative | Subject + **verb (past form)** | She visited Rome last year. |
| ❌ Negative | Subject + **didn't** + verb (base) | He didn't call me yesterday. |
| ❓ Question | **Did** + subject + verb (base)? | Did they arrive on time? |

---

## 📖 Regular vs Irregular Verbs

**Regular verbs** add -ed or -d to the base form:

> work → **worked** | play → **played** | live → **lived** | study → **studied**

**Irregular verbs** have unique past forms that must be memorized:

| Base Form | Past Simple | Base Form | Past Simple |
| --------- | ----------- | --------- | ----------- |
| go        | went        | have      | had         |
| see       | saw         | come      | came        |
| do        | did         | make      | made        |
| take      | took        | write     | wrote       |
| buy       | bought      | think     | thought     |
| run       | ran         | be        | was/were    |
	
---

## 🧠 When to Use the Past Simple

- A completed action at a **specific time** in the past.
  > *I watched a film last night.*
  > *She graduated in 2019.*

- A **sequence of past events** (one after another).
  > *He woke up, had breakfast, and left the house.*

- **Past habits or routines** (often with *used to*).
  > *We used to play football every Sunday.*
  > *She walked to school every day when she was a child.*

- A past **state or condition**.
  > *He was very shy as a teenager.*

---

## ⏰ Key Time Expressions

| Expression | Example |
|---|---|
| yesterday | I called her yesterday. |
| last week / month / year | They moved last year. |
| ... ago | She left two hours ago. |
| in + year | He was born in 1990. |
| when (+ past clause) | When I was young, I lived in Rio. |
| in the morning / evening | He read the news in the morning. |

---

## ❓ Spelling Rules for -ed Verbs

- Most verbs: just add **-ed** → work → work**ed**
- Verbs ending in **-e**: add just **-d** → live → liv**ed**
- Verbs ending in consonant + **-y**: change y to **-ied** → study → studi**ed**
- Short verbs (CVC pattern): double the consonant → stop → stop**ped**

---

## 💬 Extended Examples

> ✅ *The nurse administered the medication immediately.*
> ❌ *The patient didn't complain about the pain.*
> ❓ *Did the doctor arrive before the crisis?*
> ✅ *They signed the contract and closed the deal.*
> ✅ *She studied law for five years and became a judge.*

---

> ⚠️ **Note:** Do NOT use the Past Simple when the exact time is unknown or unimportant. In that case, use the Present Perfect.
> - ❌ *I read that book.* (with no time given)
> - ✅ *I have read that book.*
