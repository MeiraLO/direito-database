## Phrasal Verbs — by Particle

> 💡 *How to memorise:* learn them in context sentences, not just as isolated pairs. Group by particle or by topic.

---

### OUT

| Phrasal Verb | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| find out | discover information | I need to find out what time it starts. |
| carry out | perform / execute | The scientists carried out an experiment. |
| run out (of) | have no more of something | We've run out of coffee. |
| work out | calculate / exercise / solve | I can't work out how to do this. |
| set out | begin a journey / begin to do sth | She set out early in the morning. |
| sort out | organise / resolve a problem | We need to sort out this mess. |
| turn out | happen in a particular way; prove to be | It turned out to be a great party. |
| give out | distribute; stop working | They gave out leaflets. / The engine gave out. |
| stand out | be very noticeable | Her red jacket made her stand out. |
| come out | be published / become known | His new album is coming out next month. |
| point out | draw attention to | She pointed out that he was wrong. |
| miss out (on) | fail to have an experience | I missed out on the sale. |
| put out | extinguish; cause inconvenience | They put out the fire. |
| figure out | understand / solve | I can't figure out this problem. |
| fall out (with) | argue and stop being friends | She fell out with her flatmate. |

## Collocations

> 💡 *A collocation is a pair (or group) of words that naturally go together. Learn whole chunks, not individual words.*

### Sport Collocations
- **do** yoga / gymnastics / athletics / martial arts
- **play** football / tennis / chess / a sport
- **go** swimming / cycling / skiing / running
- score a **goal** / a **point** / a **try**
- **win** a match / a race / a trophy
- **take up** a sport | **give up** a sport | **train** for an event

### Emotion Collocations (+ adverbs of degree)
- deeply upset / genuinely shocked / completely devastated
- slightly embarrassed / rather anxious / incredibly proud
- absolutely delighted / utterly exhausted / terribly disappointed

### Money Collocations
- **earn** a salary / a living | **spend** money on sth
- **save up** for sth | **waste** money | **invest** in sth
- be in **debt** | be **broke** | **afford** to do sth
- **raise** funds | cut **costs** | **charge** a fee
- **pay** a fine / a bill / a deposit / cash

### Health Collocations
- **catch** a cold | **suffer from** an illness
- **make** a recovery | **take** medication
- **lead** a healthy lifestyle | **have** a check-up
- symptoms **develop** / **worsen** / **improve**
- be **admitted to** hospital | be **discharged from** hospital

### Work / Jobs Collocations
- **apply for** a job | **land** a job | **resign from** a job
- **be promoted** | **be made redundant** | **retire**
- **take on** staff | **work** overtime | **meet** a deadline
- **go on** a business trip | **attend** a meeting
- **deal with** complaints | **handle** pressure

## Word Formation

> 💡 *For IELTS Reading and Use of English: you must change the word form to fit the sentence grammatically.*

### Common Prefixes

| Prefix | Meaning | Examples |
|--------|---------|---------|
| un- | not / reverse | unhappy, undo, unknown |
| dis- | not / reverse | disagree, disappear, dishonest |
| im- / in- / ir- / il- | not | impossible, incorrect, irregular, illegal |
| re- | again | rebuild, rewrite, reconsider |
| over- | too much | overwork, overestimate, overreact |
| under- | too little / below | underestimate, underpaid, underage |
| mis- | wrongly | misunderstand, mislead, misuse |
| pre- | before | preview, predict, prepare |
| out- | more than / surpass | outnumber, outperform |

### Common Suffixes

**→ Noun**
- -tion / -sion: education, decision, permission
- -ment: achievement, development, argument
- -ness: happiness, sadness, awareness
- -ity: creativity, ability, popularity
- -ance / -ence: performance, independence, confidence
- -er / -or: teacher, visitor, actor
- -ist: artist, scientist, journalist
- -ism: tourism, criticism, journalism

**→ Adjective**
- -ful: beautiful, useful, successful
- -less: careless, homeless, useless
- -ous: dangerous, famous, enormous
- -al: natural, musical, logical
- -ive: creative, attractive, productive
- -able / -ible: comfortable, responsible, reliable
- -ic: scientific, dramatic, historic

**→ Adverb**
- -ly: quickly, carefully, successfully
(Note: friendly, lovely = adjectives, NOT adverbs)

**→ Verb**
- -ise / -ize: modernise, realise, organise
- -en: widen, strengthen, darken, frighten
- -ify: simplify, clarify, identify

### Negative adjective prefixes — which to use:

| Prefix | Typically used with... | Examples |
|--------|----------------------|---------|
| un- | most common | unhappy, uncomfortable, unusual |
| im- | before m / p | impossible, impatient, impolite |
| in- | general | incorrect, invisible, independent |
| ir- | before r | irregular, irresponsible |
| il- | before l | illegal, illogical |
| dis- | states/qualities | dishonest, disloyal, dissatisfied |
